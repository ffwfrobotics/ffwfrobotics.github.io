# Integrations

Each project stands on its own. These pages cover what happens when you
put two or three of them together — the contracts between them, and the
failure modes that only exist at the seam.

The three pairwise pages are independent; read whichever seam you are standing
on. The full-stack page is not — it assumes the other three and describes only
what the combination adds.

The seams are where these projects are least finished, so every page states
what it is claiming and on what evidence — in a draft note at the top, and for
most of them in a closing section that separates what has actually run from
what is design of record. If you are deciding whether to build on one of these,
read that part first.
